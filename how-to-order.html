How to Order

From click to delivery

Simple enough for a first order.

  1. 1

    Create your Lovegobuy account

    2 min

    Sign up with our referral link to unlock 500€ in shipping coupons instantly. Without our link, you won't get the bonus. Tip: create a new account for each big order to get fresh coupons every time.

    Create account →
  2. 2

    Find your item on Lovegofinds

    Browse the catalog, search, or filter by category until something catches your eye.

  3. 3

    Add it to your cart on Lovegobuy

    Click the item and you'll be redirected to its page on Lovegobuy. Choose your size, colour and quantity, then add it to your cart.

  4. 4

    Pay for your item

    2–5 days

    Once paid, sellers ship it to the warehouse. Delivery time varies by seller, usually 2 to 5 days.

  5. 5

    Pay for shipping

    ~10 days

    Once everything's in the warehouse, pick your shipping line and apply your coupons. Takes about 10 days depending on the line you choose.

  6. 6

    Track your delivery

    Follow your package in real time with 17track, every step of the way to your door.
