Privacy policy

1. Who we are

LovegoFinds (lovegofinds.com) lists products available on Lovegobuy and links to them. We don't sell, make or ship any products, and we don't ask for your name, email or address.

2. Data we use

  • Saved on your device: your language, currency, favorites and recently viewed items. They stay in your browser (local storage) and are never sent to us.
  • Statistics: pages viewed, country, type of device and clicks on products, measured with Google Analytics (Men and Women pages, uses cookies) and Vercel Web Analytics. This data is anonymous and only used to improve the site.
  • AI assistant: your messages are sent to Google (Gemini) to generate the answer. Questions are also saved without any personal details (emails, phone and order numbers are removed) to improve the answers. Please don't write personal information in it.
  • Parcel tracking: the tracking number you enter is sent to 17TRACK to get its status.

3. Third-party services

  • Vercel: hosting and statistics
  • Google Analytics: statistics
  • Google Gemini: AI assistant
  • 17TRACK: parcel tracking
  • Google Fonts: fonts
  • Lovegobuy: where product links lead, with its own privacy policy

4. How long data is kept

  • Saved on your device: until you delete it in your browser.
  • Google Analytics: up to 14 months.
  • Vercel Web Analytics: no cookies, anonymous visit data only.
  • AI assistant: questions without personal details, kept to improve the answers.
  • Parcel tracking: not stored by LovegoFinds.

5. Your rights (GDPR)

You can ask to access, correct or delete data about you, or object to its use. Since we don't collect names or emails, most data can't be linked to you. For any request, contact us on Discord. You can also block analytics cookies in your browser settings.
