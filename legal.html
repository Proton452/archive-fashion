Privacy & Terms

How LovegoFinds works, what data the site uses and who runs it.

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Privacy policy

LovegoFinds (lovegofinds.com) is an independent website that lists products available on Lovegobuy. We don't ask for your name, email or address, and we don't sell any data.

Statistics

To understand how the site is used, we measure visits with Google Analytics (Men and Women pages) and Vercel Web Analytics: pages viewed, country, type of device and clicks on products. Google Analytics uses cookies. This data is anonymous and only used to improve the site.

AI assistant

The messages you write in the AI assistant are sent to Google (Gemini) to generate the answer. Please don't write personal information in it.

Parcel tracking

When you track a parcel, the tracking number you enter is sent to 17TRACK to get its status.

Saved on your device

Your language, currency, favorites and recently viewed items are saved in your browser only (local storage). We never receive them, and you can delete them at any time in your browser settings.

Links to Lovegobuy

Product links take you to lovegobuy.com, whose own privacy policy applies. They include our invitation code (see Affiliate links below).

Your rights

Under the GDPR, you can ask to access or delete data about you, or object to its use. Since we don't collect names or emails, most data can't be linked to you. For any request, contact us on Discord. You can also block analytics cookies in your browser settings.
